### Melhorias

- Nova paleta de cores, em verde profundo e areia, nos temas claro e escuro. As cores de resultado — lugar, vitória e derrota — ficam como estavam, para continuarem a ler-se de relance.

## English

### Improvements

- New colour palette, in deep green and sand, across both the light and dark themes. Result colours — placement, win and loss — stay as they were, so they still read at a glance.
- Matching logo: the same design as always, with the tile in green and the bars in shades of sand.
- The animated background and the header mosaic follow the new palette.
- Primary button text in dark ink instead of white — that is what keeps enough contrast over the new gold.
